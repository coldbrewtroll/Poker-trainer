Final Table Trainer

Play WSOP-Circuit-style final tables against bots that play real solver ranges, with an ICM coach that explains every decision in prize money.

Play in your browser Download for Windows / Mac

Free. Works on iPhone and Android: open Play in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it like an app.

What's in it

250 final tables

Preset final tables with every kind of stack spread, WSOP Circuit payouts and blinds. Play until you bust or win.

Solver-range bots

Opponents play MonkerSolver ranges from 15 to 60 bb, with the final table's ICM rules for all-ins.

ICM Coach

Your equity, the pay jumps, risk premiums, jam / call / fold in dollars, and exploits: what changes against players who call or jam too wide or too tight.

Graded decisions

Every decision gets a grade, from Elite pro to Fish, and every all-in is graded in prize money.

TV-style table

Player avatars, a broadcast-style board with the pot, and win % on every all-in run-out.

Fast

Turbo speed, and Skip folds folds the hands you'd never play, so you spend your time on real decisions.

The full trainer on your computer

The desktop version adds Play mode at any depth, preflop drills, Pio flop sims, the hand tree, the leak tracker and the ICM study tools.

  1. Install Python 3.12 or newer from python.org (Windows: tick Add python.exe to PATH).
  2. Download the trainer and unzip it.
  3. Windows: double-click Poker Trainer.bat. Mac: open Terminal in the folder and run python3 trainer8.py.
